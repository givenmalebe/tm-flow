ADDENDUM 1 - TENDER E137/2025

The following changes are made to the above tender:

1. CLOSING DATE EXTENDED: The closing date has been extended from 15 December 2025 to 22 January 2026 at 11:00.

2. NEW REQUIREMENT: All bidders must now submit a valid ISO 9001 Quality Management System certificate.

3. ADDITIONAL GUARDS: The number of guards required has increased from 45 to 55 guards.

4. PRICING CHANGE: The pricing schedule must now include a separate line item for weekend rates (25% premium).

5. SCOPE CHANGE: Two additional sites have been added - Site 16 (Bellville) and Site 17 (Khayelitsha).

6. SUBMISSION CHANGE: Bids must now be submitted via the e-procurement portal at www.etenders.gov.za instead of physical delivery.

7. REMOVED REQUIREMENT: The compulsory briefing attendance is no longer mandatory. Bidders may submit questions via email instead.

8. NEW DOCUMENT: Bidders must submit a BBBEE sworn affidavit no older than 30 days.
