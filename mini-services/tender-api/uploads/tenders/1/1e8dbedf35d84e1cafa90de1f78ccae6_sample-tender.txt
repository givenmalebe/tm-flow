TENDER DOCUMENT

Tender Number: E137/2025
Tender Title: Provision of Security Guards for Municipal Facilities
Client: City of Cape Town
Closing Date: 2025-12-15
Closing Time: 11:00

SCOPE OF WORK:
The successful bidder must provide 24-hour security guarding services at 15 municipal facilities across the Western Cape. Total of 45 guards required across all sites.

PSIRA REQUIREMENTS:
- All guards must have minimum PSIRA Grade C
- Site supervisors must have PSIRA Grade B
- Project manager must have PSIRA Grade A

COMPULSORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Certified ID copies of all guards
- PSIRA registration certificates
- B-BBEE certificate or affidavit
- CIPC registration documents
- SARS tax clearance certificate
- COIDA letter of good standing
- Company profile
- 3 references from similar contracts

MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS:
- Briefing attendance is compulsory
- Minimum 3 years experience in security services
- Proof of similar contracts with municipalities

BRIEFING DETAILS:
A compulsory briefing session will be held on 2025-11-20 at 10:00 at the Civic Centre, Cape Town.

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
- Functionality: 60 points (minimum 40 to qualify)
- Price: 40 points
- B-BBEE status: preferential points

POTENTIAL DISQUALIFICATION:
- Non-attendance of compulsory briefing
- Missing compulsory documents
- Incomplete pricing schedule
- Expired PSIRA certificates

CONTRACT PERIOD: 36 months
SUBMISSION METHOD: Sealed bid deposited in the tender box at Civic Centre
